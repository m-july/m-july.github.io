MeloBottleneck DEMO:
Melody Skeleton Extraction

This page presents qualitative examples for the manuscript “MeloBottleneck: Learning Melody Skeletons as Latent Subsequences without Reduction Annotations”.

Each sample compares the original melody, the MeloBottleneck skeleton, and the O2B-Learner baseline skeleton through piano rolls and audio.

How to read the examples

The piano-roll visualization is split into a left header image and a right roll image. The header stays fixed, while the roll panel can be horizontally scrolled when the rendered sequence is longer than the viewport. Audio players below each roll provide direct listening comparisons. In the overlaid audio, the extracted skeleton is played together with the original melody.
