MeloBottleneck Demo:
Compact Symbolic Melody Representations for Robust Retrieval

This page presents qualitative examples for the manuscript “MeloBottleneck: Compact Symbolic Melody Representations for Robust Retrieval”.

Each sample compares the original melody, a MeloBottleneck skeleton, and an O2B-Learner baseline skeleton. These qualitative illustrations show the compact symbolic representations; retrieval results are reported in the paper.

How to read the examples

The piano-roll visualization is split into a left header image and a right roll image. The header stays fixed, while the roll panel can be horizontally scrolled when the rendered sequence is longer than the viewport. Audio players below each roll provide direct listening comparisons.

The displayed retention ratios are model-predicted or fixed sequence-length settings. Rounding and special-token handling can make them differ from the exact fraction of note events retained. They do not measure bitrate or file-size savings.
